# Prompt 190 — Plan Remediation

1. Confirm the problem and evidence; do not treat a hypothesis as a confirmed defect.
2. Classify it as small task, systemic remediation, design gap, contract change, new functionality, or durable decision change.
3. Require a new EDR first when accepted policy must change.
4. For REM, define exact corrective scope, exclusions, systems/files, targeted verification, rollback, phase impact, report, and approval phrase.
5. Never use REM to advance a roadmap phase or hide new scope.

Expected stop: review-ready REM or a recommendation for the correct alternative path.
