# Prompt 070 — Scoped Discovery

1. Read approved MAIN artifacts, scope registry, relevant requirements, and accepted global EDRs.
2. Explore the scope objective, current interfaces, owners, inputs/outputs, data/security concerns, failures, assumptions, and decisions/contracts needed.
3. Do not override inherited constraints or design unrelated scopes.
4. Create/update `DISC-SCOPE-001.md` under the scope directory.
5. Request `Approve Discovery SCOPE-ID v1.0` only when exit criteria pass.

Expected stop: approved or review-ready scoped discovery; no final TDD or implementation.
