# Discovery Questionnaire

Ask only relevant questions and group them into short review rounds.

1. What outcome must change, for whom, and why now?
2. Which source documents are authoritative, and who owns them?
3. What is explicitly in and out of scope?
4. What systems, users, teams, and external services participate?
5. What data enters, leaves, or persists?
6. What security, privacy, regulatory, cost, and timing constraints apply?
7. What already exists and must be preserved?
8. What failure modes are unacceptable?
9. How will stakeholders know the result is correct?
10. Does the work require one scope or several cohesive scopes?
