# Approval Gates

## Principles

- Approval must be explicit, scoped, versioned where relevant, and received after the review summary.
- Text printed inside a template or prompt is never approval.
- Completing one stage does not approve the next stage.
- Approval of a document is distinct from authorization to implement it.

## Recommended phrases

```text
Approve Discovery MAIN v1.0
Approve Scope Decomposition v1.0
Approve Requirements Baseline v1.0
Accept EDR-001
Accept Contract API-001 v1.0
Approve Solution Design SDD-MAIN-001 v1.0
Approve Technical Design TDD-SCOPE-001 v1.0
Approve Roadmap v1.0
Approve Phase 00
Approve Remediation REM-001
```

Record the approver, timestamp, artifact version, and exact approval phrase. Never infer approval from “looks good,” prior conversation, or completion of a predecessor.
