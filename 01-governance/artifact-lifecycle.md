# Artifact Lifecycle

## Planning artifacts

`Draft → Proposed → Under Review → Approved → Superseded`

## EDRs and contracts

`Draft → Proposed → Accepted → Superseded`, with `Rejected` available after review.

Accepted records are immutable. A material change creates a new version or a superseding record; history is not rewritten.

## Execution artifacts

`Planned → Awaiting Approval → Approved → In Progress → Complete`

Additional terminal or exceptional states are `Blocked`, `Cancelled`, and `Superseded`.
